System overview

Three parts, one advisory loop.

Sensors sense, the model decides, the farmer acts — and the loop feeds back every irrigation cycle.

01

Sense the field

Soil moisture, soil temperature, and ambient humidity sensors placed across representative plots stream readings at fixed intervals throughout the day.

02

Predict the need

An AI model cross-references live readings with the crop's current growth stage and short-term weather forecasts to estimate actual irrigation demand.

03

Advise the farmer

A plain-language recommendation — irrigate now, hold, or reduce — is pushed to the dashboard, replacing guesswork with a field-specific answer.
